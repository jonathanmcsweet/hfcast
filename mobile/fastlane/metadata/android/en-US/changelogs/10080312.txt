Forecasts no longer stop when the built-in sunspot record runs out, which
happened on 1 September. The record now reaches the end of 2030 and follows
the sun into its quiet years.

The map is drawn with Android's own graphics, so panning and zooming are
smoother and the download is about 15 MB smaller. On a tablet held sideways
the all-bands grid sits beside the map, and dialogs show their content again.
