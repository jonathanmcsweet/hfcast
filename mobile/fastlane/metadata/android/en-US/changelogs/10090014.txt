Android treats HFcast as a new app, so this version installs beside the old
copy rather than over it: set your station up again, then uninstall the old
one. In Obtainium, remove HFcast and add it again from the same address.

Forecasts no longer stop when the built-in sunspot record runs out, as it
did on 1 September; it now runs to the end of 2030.

The map uses Android's own graphics, so it is smoother and about 15 MB
smaller, and on a tablet held sideways the all-bands grid sits beside it.
